Why Hardware-Level Analog Beats Digital MCUs

Traditional digital circuit breakers rely on microcontrollers that suffer from boot delays, ADC conversion lag, and code crashes. This system operates purely in continuous analog space.

Instant Thermal Isolation

Direct analog feedback loop between the TMP36 sensor and LM393 comparator triggers protection relays in under 50 microseconds—stopping thermal runaway before sparks occur.
